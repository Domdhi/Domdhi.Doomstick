## Legal Basis

17 U.S.C. § 105 states:

> "Copyright protection under this title is not available for any work of the United States Government, but the United States Government is not precluded from receiving and holding copyrights transferred to it by assignment, bequest, or otherwise."

Works produced by officers or employees of the US Government as part of their official duties are not subject to copyright and are in the public domain from the moment of creation.

Pre-1928 published works (published before January 1, 1928) are in the public domain in the United States under the pre-1976 copyright framework, regardless of authorship.

## Disclaimer

This field manual is for educational and informational purposes. The information provided is accurate to the best of the authors' knowledge based on the referenced government sources, but:

- Medical and first aid information should not substitute for professional medical care.
- Plant identification errors can be fatal. Do not eat any plant without positive identification by a trained botanist or experienced forager.
- Amateur radio operation requires a valid FCC license (except in emergencies per §97.403).
- Water quality information does not substitute for laboratory testing.

When in doubt, seek professional guidance.
